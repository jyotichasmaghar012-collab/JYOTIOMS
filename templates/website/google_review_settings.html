Google Review Settings

Product reviews on this site and Google Business Reviews are two separate systems - this app never posts anything to Google automatically. After a shopper submits a product review here, they're shown a "Thank you - would you also like to review us on Google?" popup whose button just opens your Google review link below in a new tab, so they can leave that review directly on Google themselves.

Find this in Google Business Profile → Ask for reviews → Get more reviews, or Google Maps → your listing → Share → Write a review.
