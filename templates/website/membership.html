Points Program (Premium members only)

Active Premium members earn loyalty points on every order (online or in-store), based on the final amount they actually pay after all other discounts. They can redeem their points balance for a discount on any FUTURE order (never the same order that earned them). Both rates below are fully editable any time.

Example with the values above: a Premium member paying Rs. {{ '%.0f'|format(1000) }} earns {{ (1000 * points_earn_rate)|round(2) }} points, and {{ points_redeem_rate }} points can later be redeemed for Rs. 1 off a future order.
